About Dr. Lisa Pocius

As a family physician with two decades of experience, I thought I knew everything about preventive medicine. Then I was diagnosed with osteoporosis and experienced compression fractures firsthand. Osteoporosis: The Book I Wish I'd Had When I Was Diagnosed combines my medical expertise with the personal lessons from my own journey, a practical, compassionate guide for real people navigating this diagnosis.
