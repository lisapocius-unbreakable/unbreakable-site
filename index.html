About Lisa Pocius, MD

I spent ten years in family practice and believed I understood osteoporosis. Then I fell in my own front yard at 55 and fractured four vertebrae. The diagnosis came afterward, and so did the discovery of how much the textbook leaves out. My first book is what I put together in the months that followed, written for the person sitting where I sat.
