About Lisa Pocius, MD

I spent ten years in family practice and believed I understood osteoporosis. Then I fell in my own front yard at 55 and fractured four vertebrae. The diagnosis came afterward, and so did the discovery of how much the textbook leaves out. Osteoporosis: The Book I Wish I'd Had When I Was Diagnosed is what I put together in the months that followed, written for the person sitting where I sat. Read more about me →
