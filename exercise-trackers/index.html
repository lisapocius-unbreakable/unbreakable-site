Printable logs and trackers

These are the tracking pages from Osteoporosis Exercises for Beginners, ready to print. They're free, and you don't need to sign up for anything.

Download the PDF 4 pages, letter size, about 65 KB. It prints cleanly in black and white.
By Lisa Pocius, MD  ·  physician and osteoporosis patient

What's in the PDF

Print the Daily Movement Log as often as you need, one copy per week. The other pages last the whole 12 weeks and beyond.

The log page is set up landscape. Most printers turn it automatically. If yours doesn't, choose landscape for that page in the print settings.

General education, not medical advice. If something feels wrong during a session, stop and check with your doctor or physical therapist.

The diagnosis of osteoporosis came afterward, and so did the discovery of how much the textbook leaves out.
