The Calcium and Protein Cheat Sheet for Bone Health

A one-page reference of the best food sources for calcium and protein, plus daily targets and the four foods that give you both in one bite. Fridge-worthy and physician-vetted.

By Lisa Pocius, MD  ·  physician and osteoporosis patient

What's on the page

  • Daily targets for calcium (1,200 mg), protein, and vitamin D at a glance
  • Four powerhouse foods that deliver meaningful calcium and protein in a single serving
  • Twelve top calcium foods with realistic serving sizes, from dairy to plant-based
  • Twelve top protein foods across meat, fish, dairy, legumes, and eggs
  • The gotchas most guides skip: the 500 mg absorption cap, why spinach isn't the calcium hero you think, and why plant milks vary so much

Who this is for

If you've been told to eat more calcium or more protein and you're staring at a grocery list wondering where to start, this is the page I wish I'd had when I was diagnosed. It pulls the food tables from Chapter 8 of my book into one printable sheet you can stick on the fridge.

It works for you if you've got osteoporosis or osteopenia, if a parent or spouse does, or if you're just trying to eat in a way that protects your skeleton for the decades ahead.

About the author

I spent ten years in family practice before I fell in my own front yard at 55 and fractured four vertebrae. The diagnosis of osteoporosis came afterward, and so did the discovery of how much the textbook leaves out. I wrote Osteoporosis: The Book I Wish I'd Had When I Was Diagnosed for the person sitting where I sat.
